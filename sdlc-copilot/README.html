1 · What we are doing

Establishes the framework's purpose and boundaries before the team discusses implementation details.

It is not an application. There is no server, no runtime, no package install, and no Salesforce deployment.

It is a repository of instructions and organizational context that GitHub Copilot can read. The repo defines slash commands, the method behind those commands, and the facts the model is allowed to use for this organization.

We are creating a source-controlled delivery playbook for Salesforce and nCino work. Prompts expose commands, skills encode repeatable judgment, and context prevents the model from inventing policy.

The goal

Give every request the same intake discipline, architecture assessment, story quality, development handoff, QE evidence, promotion readiness, UAT record, and release handoff — without moving human accountability into the model.

2 · Why the repo matters

Makes the operating model tangible: developers can inspect, review, version, and extend the framework through a familiar repository structure.

The easiest way to understand the framework is to read the tree. Each folder has a narrow job, and the separation makes the system inspectable for developers, team leads, managers, and reviewers.

3 · How files become behavior

Shows how prompts, skills, and context work together without mixing tool wiring, delivery method, and organizational truth.

Prompt

.github/prompts/sdlc-po-intake.prompt.md puts /sdlc-po-intake in the Copilot menu. It should stay short: tell Copilot which skill to use, what input is required, and what not to do.

Skill

.github/skills/sdlc-po-intake/SKILL.md is the method. It defines the trigger, pre-flight checks, phases, output shape, prohibited actions, and escalation rules.

Context

sdlc-copilot/context/org-guardrails.md, org-profile.md, and estimation-rubric.md provide organization-specific facts. Skills reference these by path rather than copying their content.

The design principle

Prompts are tool wiring. Skills are method. Context is governed truth. When those are mixed together, the framework becomes harder to review, harder to migrate, and easier to accidentally corrupt.

Current readiness

All eleven persona stages have a base skill and prompt. Three are active reference implementations; eight are foundation skills for their owning teams to deepen. The context files are intentionally unpopulated, so this release is ready for team review and configuration, not live organizational delivery.

4 · The plan: intake through production

Connects every persona and handoff into one visible delivery path, from the first business request through production support.

The rollout spine covers the full delivery path: five persona namespaces, eleven owned skills, and a visible handoff from business intake all the way to release. Some skills are deeper than others today, but every stage has a discoverable folder, prompt, owner namespace, and handoff target.

5 · Human accountable, human verifiable, human in the loop

Allows AI to accelerate the work while named people retain ownership, verify evidence, and make every material decision.

AI can draft, analyze, organize, and recommend. It cannot own an outcome, accept risk, make a business commitment, or approve a release. Every skill names the human role accountable for the outcome, the role that verifies the work, the gate that must be decided, and the evidence needed to decide it.

ControlWhy it is requiredHow v1 implements it
Human accountableOwnership and risk cannot be transferred to a model.Each skill declares accountable_role.
Human verifiablePolished output can still be incomplete or wrong.Each output records sources, assumptions, gaps, and verification_evidence.
Human in the loopMaterial handoffs need a real decision, not implied approval.AI leaves every human_gate Pending; only the named verifier may accept or reject.
Keep the loop meaningful

Human-in-the-loop does not mean adding a ceremonial review after every AI action. It means a named person verifies evidence and decides at the handoffs where scope, quality, risk, or release state changes.

The framework also refuses continuation rather than generating confident artifacts on weak foundations. Stop conditions are part of the delivery control.

  1. Intake will not qualify a request missing data classification, customer-data scope, or regulatory driver.
  2. Assessment will not start on an unqualified brief, or when the required sdlc-copilot/context/ files are empty.
  3. Reconciliation must produce signed scope before stories are treated as ready for development.
  4. Story work must trace back to signed scope; anything new becomes a proposed addition.
  5. Promotion, execution, UAT, and release should name owners and evidence rather than implying approval happened automatically.

6 · Appropriate context and model routing

Improves quality and token efficiency by loading only the authoritative context needed and matching model capability to task complexity.

Good results require the right context in the right amount. The target is not simply fewer tokens. It is context that is relevant, current, authoritative, and sufficient for the decision being made.

Loading layerWhat loadsPurpose
Always on.github/copilot-instructions.mdShort non-negotiable framework rules
On matchThe selected SKILL.mdMethod, controls, and output contract
On referenceFiles named by reads_contextOnly the organizational facts required by that skill
For the requestNamed project artifactsCurrent intake, design, stories, or evidence

Too little context creates assumptions and missed controls. Too much can add stale or conflicting facts, expose unnecessary data, and spend tokens without improving the result. Skills should never load an entire project or context library "just in case."

Three durable model tiers

TierTypical workGuidance
efficientExtraction, classification, formatting, routine summariesUse Auto or an approved lightweight model; escalate when ambiguity or risk is material.
balancedMost workflow, planning, implementation, and review workUse Copilot Auto by default.
deep-reasoningCompeting designs, cross-system ambiguity, high-consequence tradeoffsUse Auto or a reasoning model validated on representative work.
Why prompts do not pin a model

Model availability, price, and performance change. Exact models should be pinned only when representative evaluation demonstrates a material quality benefit. The skill declares a stable tier; context/model-routing.md holds the routing policy and review cadence.

7 · How we are implementing it

Turns the vision into a practical, file-based approach teams can adopt inside the development repository they already use.

The implementation is intentionally incremental and file-based. We are not asking teams to adopt a new application. We are adding governed skills and context files to a repo they can inspect, review, version, and extend.

Implementation principles

  • Start with the delivery spine. The handoff order is visible in sdlc-copilot/SKILLS.md and each skill's input_from / output_to fields.
  • Keep prompts thin. Prompts launch commands. Skills hold the method.
  • Keep facts in context. Org guardrails, delivery standards, security rules, and release expectations live in human-owned context files.
  • Let each persona own its namespace. PO, architecture, dev, QE, and Ops teams extend the framework where they own the work.
  • Validate every change. The validator catches discovery failures that Copilot would otherwise fail silently.

Folder by folder

.github/ — what Copilot reads

PathPurposeStatus
.github/copilot-instructions.md Always-on repository instructions. Keep this very short because it loads on every turn for every user. built
.github/prompts/*.prompt.md Slash-command launchers. Thin files only; no delivery method belongs here. built
.github/skills/*/SKILL.md The method library. Must stay flat because Copilot scans immediate child folders for SKILL.md. built
.github/workflows/validate.yml Runs the validator on pull requests so broken skill discovery fails in review, not after rollout. built

sdlc-copilot/context/ — the facts that make it yours

PathWhat belongs thereStatus
org-guardrails.md Always/never rules, capability disposition register, exception process. Highest priority to populate. skeleton
org-profile.md Salesforce clouds, nCino usage, delivery model, environments, promotion path, systems of record, integration landscape. skeleton
estimation-rubric.md ROM ranges, complexity multipliers, confidence criteria, and actuals feedback loop. skeleton
dev-standards.md, qe-standards.md, release-standards.md Persona-owned definitions of done, required evidence, test expectations, promotion gates, and production handoff requirements. skeleton
security-and-access.md, ncino-boundaries.md Security, permission, managed-package, and extension boundaries used during design, implementation, and review. skeleton
model-routing.md Efficient, balanced, and deep-reasoning tiers; Auto default; evaluation and review rules for any pinned-model exception. control

Why skeleton context is intentional

A blank guardrail fails loudly. A plausible AI-written guardrail fails quietly and later gets cited as if it were approved policy. The context files need named human owners.

8 · Installing it in the Salesforce repository

Gives every developer the same framework version through the shared Salesforce repository, without asking each person to assemble it locally.

The framework repository is the upstream source. The Salesforce repository is the consumer. A framework owner imports an approved framework version through a pull request; developers then receive it through their normal Salesforce repository clone and pull workflow.

One shared installation

Individual developers should not copy or merge the framework into separate local clones. One owner integrates it into the shared Salesforce repository, and the team reviews and versions that installation like any other repository change.

Target repository layout

salesforce-repository/
├── .github/                 existing GitHub files plus Copilot prompts and skills
├── sdlc-copilot/            framework context, docs, projects, templates, validation
├── force-app/               Salesforce source code
├── manifest/
└── ...

Initial import

Clone the two repositories beside each other. Check out an approved framework tag or commit, copy sdlc-copilot/, and merge the required .github/ content into the Salesforce repository.

workspace/
├── ai-sdlc-framework/
└── salesforce-repository/

git clone FRAMEWORK_REPOSITORY_URL ai-sdlc-framework
git clone SALESFORCE_REPOSITORY_URL salesforce-repository
git -C ai-sdlc-framework checkout APPROVED_TAG_OR_COMMIT

rsync -a ai-sdlc-framework/sdlc-copilot/ \
  salesforce-repository/sdlc-copilot/
rsync -a ai-sdlc-framework/.github/prompts/ \
  salesforce-repository/.github/prompts/
rsync -a ai-sdlc-framework/.github/skills/ \
  salesforce-repository/.github/skills/

Do not replace an existing .github/ folder. Merge and review copilot-instructions.md, CODEOWNERS, and workflows so current Salesforce repository governance is preserved. Run the framework validator, inspect the full diff, record the imported framework commit in the PR, and merge only after the persona and repository owners approve it.

What Git should track

Path or contentTrack?Reason
.github/prompts/, .github/skills/, and approved Copilot instructions YesCopilot discovers them from the repository, and every developer needs the same version.
sdlc-copilot/Yes This is the shared framework, context structure, contribution guidance, and validator.
Approved project metadata and non-sensitive artifactsBy policy Commit only what the organization permits; link to the approved system of record for everything else.
Temporary source clone, scratch files, exports, secrets, and local editor stateNo These are installation inputs or machine-local data, not part of the shared framework.
Do not gitignore the installed framework

Ignoring .github/ framework files or sdlc-copilot/ would make the installation local, invisible to review, and inconsistent across developers. If a temporary framework clone must sit inside the Salesforce working tree, ignore only that staging folder, such as .sdlc-framework-source/, preferably in the developer's local .git/info/exclude. Keeping the repositories beside each other avoids the issue entirely.

Ongoing updates

  1. Framework owners publish an approved tag or commit from the framework repository.
  2. One synchronization PR updates the tracked framework files in the Salesforce repository.
  3. Owners review the diff for prompts, skills, context, workflows, and handoff changes.
  4. Developers pull normally. No second repository or manual merge is required in their daily workflow.

9 · How teams add to the framework

Makes extension ownership explicit so each persona can improve its methods without weakening shared handoffs or controls.

The contribution model is intentionally file-based. A team should be able to add a skill by copying the template, naming it correctly, declaring the handoff, and passing validation.

mkdir -p .github/skills/sdlc-qe-test-plan
cp sdlc-copilot/template/SKILL.md \
   .github/skills/sdlc-qe-test-plan/SKILL.md
cp sdlc-copilot/template/example.prompt.md \
   .github/prompts/sdlc-qe-test-plan.prompt.md

Then edit:
.github/skills/sdlc-qe-test-plan/SKILL.md
.github/prompts/sdlc-qe-test-plan.prompt.md
sdlc-copilot/SKILLS.md

bash sdlc-copilot/scripts/validate-skills.sh

Rules for extensions

  • Use the persona namespace. Examples: sdlc-dev-*, sdlc-qe-*, sdlc-ops-*. The prefix means ownership, not permission.
  • Do not change the shared spine casually. Existing handoff contracts, copilot-instructions.md, persona prefixes, and sdlc-copilot/context/ are shared surfaces.
  • Write descriptions as routing rules. The description should say when to use the skill and when not to use it. That text drives selection.
  • Add prohibitions and escalation. A skill that cannot say what it must not do is not finished.

10 · What success looks like

Defines observable adoption and delivery outcomes so the framework is improved from evidence rather than enthusiasm alone.

The objective is not automation for automation's sake. The objective is to improve the quality, consistency, and traceability of work as it moves through delivery.

1 Same input discipline Requests get qualified the same way, including data classification, customer-data scope, hard dates, and sign-off authority.
2 Same assessment structure Architecture produces a design, rejected alternative, ROM range, assumptions, confidence, and governance routing.
3 Same backlog handoff Stories trace to signed scope, then flow into technical breakout, build scaffolding, test automation, promotion, execution, UAT, and release.
The development-team value

Developers should receive fewer vague stories, fewer hidden assumptions, and fewer late discoveries about data, integrations, governance, and scope.

Integration examples, not vendor snapshots

12 · What this deliberately does not do

Prevents misuse by separating AI assistance from approval, policy authority, production decisions, and human ownership.

Not builtWhy
Bundled vendor implementations The included sf-skills-* entries are wrappers. Import and review upstream Salesforce skill folders and their resources before using them as implementation engines.
Approval or risk acceptance The framework can name the human decision needed. It cannot make the decision.
Automatic QE or release decisions The spine structures evidence and names decision owners. QE, business, release, and support owners retain their existing authority.
Enterprise policy storage Policy belongs in its system of record. This repo should point to it or summarize approved guardrails with ownership and review cadence.

13 · Rollout path

Provides a concrete sequence for moving from a reviewed framework to an owned, configured, and exercised team capability.

StepOwnerOutcome
Assign owners and repository controls Framework owner + persona leads Replace every TBD, configure CODEOWNERS, and protect shared surfaces.
Populate approved context Architecture + Dev + QE + Ops owners Skills can operate without inventing organizational facts or standards.
Confirm project artifact boundaries Framework owner + security/records owners Teams know what may be committed under projects/ and what remains a link to an approved system of record.
Run one real request end to end All five personas Validate all eleven handoffs against actual delivery work and revise from evidence.

Adoption test

During rollout, at least one useful skill should be deepened by someone outside the core framework authors. If that does not happen, the extension model needs work.
